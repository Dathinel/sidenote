# Code of Conduct

Sidenote follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That text is the full policy. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback with care. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Do not harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private.
- Do not publish other people's private information, including meeting content.
- Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break these rules, and may
  block people who keep breaking them.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

To report a problem, use one of these:

- The private [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/harshanandak/sidenote/security/advisories/new)
  form under the repository's Security tab. It is private to you and the maintainer,
  so you can use it for conduct reports too.
- The maintainer's GitHub profile: [harshanandak](https://github.com/harshanandak).

Reports are kept private. The maintainer looks into each report and decides on any
action using the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
