# Delete a meeting

Open the meeting's More actions menu or the More menu on its library row,
choose **Delete meeting**, and confirm the named meeting. It disappears
from Meetings, search, tasks and new Ask context on this device.
Folder sync may affect copies on other devices.
Delete is available on Windows.
Delete requires a connection to Hex so Sidenote can confirm the meeting has stopped recording.

Choose **Undo** in the confirmation toast to restore and reopen the meeting.
The **Deleted meetings** button in the library lists recoverable meetings
with their deletion date and days left. Sidenote clears expired app data
when it starts after the 30-day restore period.

Hex recordings stay in place. Exports, earlier Ask answers, daily-check
history and accepted memory are kept. A daily check that started before
the deletion can still finish and save its report. A meeting copied from
another device can be deleted on its recording device.
