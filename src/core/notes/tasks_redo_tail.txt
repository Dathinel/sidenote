# Output format
Extract concrete tasks from the meeting, with short verb-led titles and useful descriptions. Do not sort projects in this response. This output format takes priority over project sorting instructions above.
Reply with {"todo":[{"title":"Review the sample","description":"What needs doing and why.","status":"todo","waiting_on":null,"subtasks":[],"team":null,"owner":null,"due":null,"at":[]}]}
- Every task needs title. Use at most 6 words and 45 characters.
- status is todo, waiting or review. waiting_on is null, {"ref":"another task ref"} or {"text":"a person or event"}.
- Tasks may have a ref for references in waiting_on. Only reference a task in this meeting.
- Subtasks are usually empty. Add them only when the meeting itself listed separate steps for this to-do. Never split one action into steps yourself. At most 4.
- team names must come from Teams. Never invent a person, date or task.
- description is 1 to 3 plain sentences. at holds transcript timestamps when available.
