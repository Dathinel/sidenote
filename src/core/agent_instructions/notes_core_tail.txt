# Output format

Reply with exactly one JSON object with these fields. All text values are plain sentences: no Markdown, no bullets, no timestamps inside text.

{
  "title": "At most 6 words naming what this meeting was about",
  "kind": "A short phrase, for example Weekly team call, Customer call, Interview, Webinar",
  "people": ["Names of people who clearly took part; [] when unknown"],
  "context": ["2 to 4 short points: why the meeting happened and what it set out to settle"],
  "topics": [
    {"title": "A specific topic name, never just Discussion",
     "points": [{"type": "fact", "text": "One short sentence", "who": "Name, or leave out", "at": ["00:12:30"]}]}
  ],
  "todo": [
    {"ref": "t1",
     "title": "Verb first, at most 6 words",
     "description": "1 to 3 short sentences",
     "status": "todo",
     "waiting_on": null,
     "subtasks": [],
     "owner": "A name, You, or null",
     "team": "A name from the Teams list, or null",
     "due": "YYYY-MM-DD or null",
     "at": ["00:12:58"],
     "span": ["00:11:40", "00:13:05"]}
  ],
  "to_settle": [{"question": "One sentence", "waits_on": "A name, You, or null"}],
  "verify": ["One short line each"]
}

Field limits:
- point type is one of: fact, decided, concern, response, open.
- status is todo or waiting. waiting_on is null, {"ref": "<another item's ref>"} or {"text": "<a person or event>"}.
- Subtasks are usually empty. Add them only when the meeting itself listed separate steps for this to-do. Never split one action into steps yourself. At most 4.
- team: only a name from the Teams list, else null. Never invent a team.
- at and span use timestamps that appear in the transcript: at most 2 per point, exactly 1 per to-do in at, and span is [start, end] around it. When the transcript has no timestamps, use [] everywhere.
- refs are t1, t2, t3 and so on, unique in this reply.
